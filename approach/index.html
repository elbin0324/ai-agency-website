03Security and data

How we handle access to your business.

The practices we follow on every engagement, in plain words. Send your security questionnaire and we’ll answer it in writing.

  • Only the keys each job needs

    Each agent gets its own credentials and the minimum access its job requires (least privilege), read-only wherever possible. We review access regularly and remove it when it’s no longer needed.

  • Your data doesn’t train AI

    We build on business-grade AI services whose terms exclude training on your data. Before anything connects, you get a list of every outside service that will touch it.

  • A short list of people

    Only the people building and running your agents can see your data, under a signed confidentiality agreement. We review agent work inside your systems, not in copies.

  • Only the data a job needs

    We connect the minimum a workflow requires, avoid copying data out of your systems and don’t keep what we don’t need.

  • Rules for sensitive data

    Payment details, health information and personal records are masked, excluded or kept in the systems built for them. We agree the rules with whoever handles compliance for you before anything connects.

  • Passwords in a vault

    Passwords and keys are kept in a secure vault and never shared over email or chat.

  • A record of everything

    Every agent action is logged: what happened, when and why. The audit log is yours to read at any time.

  • Told the same day

    If something goes wrong, we pause the affected agent, tell you the same business day, fix the cause and give you a written account of what changed.

  • A clean exit

    When an engagement ends, we remove our access, hand over the documentation and delete working copies of your data on request.

About certifications. We don’t have a SOC 2 report yet, and we won’t imply otherwise. What you see above are the practices we follow, not certifications or guarantees. In a regulated field, we scope data handling with whoever handles compliance for you before anything connects.

04Human in the loop

Agents earn autonomy. You decide how much.

Every agent starts with people approving its work. It takes on more only when its track record supports it, and only when you say so.

Four levels of autonomy

  1. Level 1

    Draft

    The agent prepares the work. A person sends, books or changes it.

    A person acts

  2. Level 2

    Approve

    The agent acts once a person approves each item.

    Your OK on each item

  3. Level 3

    Spot-check

    The agent handles routine work on its own. People review a sample and every exception.

    You review samples

  4. Level 4

    Within limits

    The agent handles routine work end to end, inside hard limits. Anything outside them goes to a person.

    Exceptions come to you

You can move any agent down a level, or pause it, at any time.

Controls you keep

  • Approval rules

    Choose what needs a yes: by category, dollar amount, customer type or anything else.

  • Hard limits

    Spending caps, send limits, discount and refund ceilings, and hours the agent can’t exceed.

  • Escalation rules

    Complaints, legal or medical questions, safety issues, key customers and large orders always go to a person.

  • Clear handoffs

    When an agent passes something to a person, it includes the full context and a summary.

  • Quality reviews

    We review samples of agent work weekly at first, then monthly once things are stable, and track accuracy over time.

  • An off switch

    Pause any agent at any time, and your team picks up where it left off.

06FAQ

Questions about working with us.

Something else? Bring it to your free assessment call.

How much does it cost?

We don’t publish prices because scope drives them, but here’s how pricing works. A pilot is a fixed price for a fixed scope, agreed in writing before any work starts. If you continue, managed agents are a flat monthly fee that covers monitoring, upkeep, changes within scope and AI usage at the volumes we agreed. Third-party costs, like phone minutes or a software seat, are listed in the proposal at cost.

There’s no long-term contract to start, and every proposal puts our fee next to the modeled savings, so you see the payback before you sign. Want a yardstick now? The savings calculator estimates what your team’s time is worth each month: the most an AI program could cost and still pay for itself.

How long until it’s working?

A first agent is typically live 2 to 4 weeks after kickoff. For the first few weeks, your team approves its work in short reviews built into the day, and that’s how it learns your standards. Some benefits, like answering after-hours inquiries, start on day one. Time savings grow as you let routine work through without review, and the full benefit usually takes 2 to 3 months. Projects that connect to practice-management, field-service or ERP systems take longer, and you’ll get a dated plan before work starts.

What if an agent gets something wrong?

It will happen occasionally, so we plan for it. Agents answer only from information you approve, anything unclear goes to a person, and early on your team approves the work before it goes out, which is where most mistakes get caught.

If one slips through, we tell you the same business day, correct it with the customer or in the record, pause that kind of task if needed and fix the cause. You’ll see what happened and what changed in the next report. Your agreement spells out who is responsible for what, and every action is logged, so there’s never a question about what the agent did or why.

Do you have a SOC 2 report?

Not yet, and we won’t imply otherwise. Instead, we answer your security questionnaire in writing, give you a list of every outside service that will touch your data, and work within your IT policies, in accounts you own wherever possible. See how we handle access and data.

Who will we work with?

The people who design and build your agents. You get one named lead who knows your business and answers your questions directly. There’s no handoff from a sales team to a support queue.

What if the pilot misses its target?

We tell you plainly, explain why and recommend whether to adjust the approach, try a different job or stop. You decide, and you’re under no obligation to continue.

Who owns what you build?

Your data and accounts stay yours, and so does the business knowledge we organize for your agents, like FAQs, price lists and rules. We set things up in accounts you own wherever possible and document how everything works. Ownership terms are spelled out in your agreement.

What if Halyard goes away?

Your agents run in accounts you own wherever possible, and everything is documented as we go. If we stop working together for any reason, we hand over the documentation and credentials, remove our access and follow the exit steps written into your agreement.

Can you work with our IT provider or marketing agency?

Yes. We coordinate access with your IT provider and keep them informed about what we connect. The Marketing Director works alongside your agency, sharing findings and test results.

Do you train our team?

Yes. Short, practical sessions on reviewing agent work, changing rules and handling handoffs, plus a one-page guide for each agent.
