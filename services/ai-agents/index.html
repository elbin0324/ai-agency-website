01Customer Agent

Every customer answered, day or night.

It answers on your website, by text, by email and on the phone, from your own knowledge. It books the work and hands off to your team with the full story.

Introduces itself as a virtual assistant. Customers can ask for a person at any time.

What it does

  • Answers from your knowledge

    Learns your services, policies, service area, hours and FAQs. If it doesn't know, it says so and asks your team instead of guessing.

  • Works every channel

    Website chat, text, email and phone, with one conversation history per customer.

  • Quotes from your price book

    Prices standard jobs and products by your rules. Custom work gets a range or goes to your team.

  • Books the work

    Checks real availability in your calendar or scheduling system, books the slot and sends the confirmation.

  • Handles orders and returns

    Takes orders and job details, answers “where’s my order?” from your store and handles returns within your policy.

  • Qualifies leads

    Asks the questions your best salesperson would, then routes hot leads to the right person right away.

  • Follows up

    Texts back missed calls, follows up on open quotes, sends reminders and asks for a review after the job.

  • Hands off with the full story

    When a customer asks for a person, gets upset or needs something outside its rules, your team takes over with the transcript and a summary.

  • Speaks your customers’ language

    Replies in the language the customer writes in, including Spanish.

Guardrails and quality

The rules it follows in every conversation.

How human approval works

  • Approved knowledge only

    It answers from sources you approve. No guessing at prices, policies or promises.

  • Handoff rules you set

    Complaints, refunds, VIP customers, legal or medical questions and large orders go to a person.

  • Safety first

    A gas smell, a flood or a medical emergency gets safety instructions and goes straight to a person.

  • Hard limits

    Discounts, refunds and order sizes it can’t exceed, whatever a customer asks.

02Analyst Agent

Your numbers, cleaned up and explained.

It reads your books, untangles messy spreadsheets, builds the workbooks and tells you in plain English what changed and why.

Works alongside your bookkeeper and accountant, not instead of them. Read-only access to your books by default.

What it does

  • Reads the books

    Works from QuickBooks and Xero exports, bank and card statements, invoices and payroll summaries.

  • Cleans messy data

    Fixes duplicates, inconsistent names, broken dates and merged cells, and logs every change it makes.

  • Builds the workbooks

    Formulas, lookups and pivot tables your team can follow and edit. No black boxes.

  • Charts and dashboards

    KPI dashboards in Excel or Google Sheets that refresh on a schedule.

  • Forecasts

    Cash flow, revenue, inventory and staffing forecasts, with every assumption written down.

  • Explains what changed

    A plain-English summary: what moved, why it likely moved and what to look at next.

  • Flags what needs attention

    Unusual expenses, duplicate payments, slipping margins and overdue invoices, before they become problems.

  • Answers questions

    Ask “What was our margin on commercial jobs last quarter?” and get the number, the source and the method.

  • Reconciles across systems

    Matches CRM deals to invoices, orders to shipments and inventory counts to sales.

04FAQ

Questions about AI agents.

Straight answers on disclosure, mistakes, your software and your data. Don’t see yours? Ask us on the free assessment call.

Will customers know they’re talking to AI?

Yes. The Customer Agent introduces itself as a virtual assistant, and customers can ask for a person at any time. Being upfront builds trust, and in some places it’s required.

What happens when it doesn’t know the answer?

It says so, tells the customer what happens next and passes the question to the right person with the full conversation. If you want it to handle that question next time, we add the answer to its approved knowledge.

What if an agent gets something wrong?

It will happen occasionally, so we plan for it. Agents answer only from information you approve, anything unclear goes to a person, and early on your team approves the work before it goes out, which is where most mistakes get caught.

If one slips through, we correct it, pause that kind of task if needed and fix the cause, and you’ll see what happened in the next report. Every action is logged, so there’s never a question about what the agent did or why. How human approval works.

Can it work with our scheduling, field-service or practice software?

Often, yes. If your system has an API or a reliable integration, the agent reads real availability and books directly. If not, we set up a practical workaround, such as holding the slot on a shared calendar for your team to confirm. Either way, you’ll know which applies, and how much of your team’s time it takes, before any work starts.

Can you work with patient information?

Yes, with the right paperwork and tight limits. Before an agent touches patient information, we sign a business associate agreement (BAA) with your practice, and every AI and messaging service that handles that information has signed one with us. Agents get the narrowest access the job needs, usually scheduling and patient messaging, not clinical charts, and clinical questions always go to your team. Every action is logged.

We set this up with whoever handles HIPAA for you, often the office manager. You won’t see a HIPAA badge here, because there’s no official HIPAA certification for vendors. Instead, we’ll show you exactly how your patients’ information is handled.

Will the Analyst Agent replace our bookkeeper or accountant?

No. Your bookkeeper and accountant keep the books accurate and compliant. The Analyst Agent takes on the reporting, cleanup and analysis around them, and it reads your accounting data without changing it.

Can the Executive Assistant send email on my behalf?

Only if you want it to. By default it drafts and you approve. You can allow auto-send for routine categories, like scheduling confirmations, and keep approval on for everything else. Folders and senders you mark private are excluded.
