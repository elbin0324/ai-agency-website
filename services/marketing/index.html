01Where budgets leak

The usual leaks, found and sized.

The first audit checks for the patterns that quietly drain ad budgets, and puts a dollar figure on each one it finds.

  1. 01

    Search terms that never convert

    Clicks from people looking for jobs, free advice or a service you don’t offer, paid for every month.

  2. 02

    Spend where it can’t pay off

    Ads shown outside the area you serve, at hours nobody can answer, or for products that are out of stock or too thin on margin to advertise.

  3. 03

    Campaigns competing with each other

    Two campaigns bidding on the same searches and driving up your own costs.

  4. 04

    Tired creative

    The same ad shown to the same people too many times, with results sliding.

  5. 05

    Landing pages that lose people

    Slow pages, unclear offers and forms that ask too much.

  6. 06

    Tracking you can’t trust

    Conversions counted twice or not at all, so every decision rests on bad data.

  7. 07

    Sales counted twice

    Google and Meta can both claim the same sale. We check their numbers against your real orders and booked jobs, and judge campaigns on margin, not revenue.

  8. Your OK

    Every finding comes with the fix, and nothing changes without your approval.

03What it does

A marketing director that tests, measures and reports.

It does the analysis a good marketing lead would do, every day, across every account.

  • Full-account audit

    Every campaign, ad group, keyword, audience and search term across Google Ads, Meta Ads, Microsoft Ads and LinkedIn.

  • Ties spend to revenue

    Connects ad data to your website analytics and CRM, so you see cost per booked job or sale, not just cost per click.

  • Fixes tracking first

    Checks that conversions are counted correctly and flags broken tags and double counting. Good decisions need good data.

  • Cuts the waste

    Adds negative keywords, fixes location and schedule settings and pauses what’s losing money, with your approval.

  • Designs experiments

    Tests one change at a time (creative, audience, offer, landing page or budget split), each with a clear hypothesis and a rule for when to stop.

  • Calls winners on evidence

    Waits for enough data before declaring a winner, so you don’t chase noise.

  • Moves budget to what works

    Shifts spend toward the campaigns bringing in customers, inside the caps and rules you approve.

  • Drafts new creative

    Writes headlines, ad copy and landing page variants in your brand voice, for your approval.

  • Reports in plain English

    A short weekly note on what changed, what we tested, what we learned and what’s next, plus a monthly view of cost per lead, cost per acquisition and return on ad spend.

05Guardrails

Your budget, your brand, your rules.

The agent works inside limits you set. The big decisions stay with you.

  • Spending caps

    Set inside the ad platforms wherever they support it. The agent can’t raise them without your approval.

  • Approval for big moves

    New campaigns, new offers, budget increases and new messaging all wait for your OK.

  • Brand-safe copy

    Every ad follows your brand voice and a list of approved claims. No invented promises, discounts or guarantees.

  • Customer data stays put

    Customer lists never go to an ad platform without your approval.

  • Change log and rollback

    Every change is logged with the reason and the result, and any change can be rolled back.

  • Platform and industry rules

    Special ad categories (housing, employment) and regulated fields (healthcare, legal) follow platform policy and your compliance review.

  • Early warnings

    If results dip or something looks off, the agent flags it right away and holds risky changes until you’ve seen it.

  • An off switch

    Pause the agent at any time. It makes no further changes until you say so.

07FAQ

Questions about the Marketing Director.

Does this replace my agency or marketer?

No. It works for them. Your marketer or agency keeps the strategy, the brand and the final say on creative and budget. The Marketing Director does the daily work they rarely have time for: checking every search term and placement, spotting tired ads, running clean tests and writing up what happened. If you don’t have a marketer, it can run the accounts day to day inside the limits you set, and the big calls still come to you.

Can it spend more than my budget?

Not without your approval. We set spending caps inside the ad platforms wherever they support it, and only you can approve an increase.

How soon will we see results?

Audit findings and quick fixes typically arrive in the first two weeks. Tests take longer. How long depends on your spend and traffic, and we won’t call a winner before the data supports it.

Which platforms do you work with?

We start with Google Ads and Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram), and also work with Microsoft Ads and LinkedIn. We connect your website analytics and CRM too, so results tie to real customers and real orders.

Do our customer lists go to the ad platforms?

Not without your approval. Some advertisers upload customer lists to find similar buyers or to measure results. That’s your decision, made with whoever handles privacy for you, and the agent never does it on its own.

Is there a minimum ad spend?

There’s no fixed minimum. At smaller budgets the biggest gains usually come from fixing tracking, cutting waste and tightening targeting, rather than from running lots of tests. We’ll tell you on the assessment call which applies to you.
