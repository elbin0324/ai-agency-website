AI Consulting

A ranked plan to cut costs and grow your margin.

A consulting-style review of your books and operations that ranks every saving, pricing gap and process fix by value, effort and payback. AI does the weeks of data work a firm would bill for; a person checks every finding.

01What we look at

Five places we look for margin.

Margin usually leaks out in small places nobody has had time to check.

  • Costs and vendors

    Every expense line, trended. Vendor price creep, overlapping subscriptions and contracts worth renegotiating.

  • Pricing and margin

    Margin by job, product, service line and customer, and where prices have fallen behind costs.

  • Time and process

    Where your team’s hours go, where work gets redone and where handoffs stall.

  • Cash

    Receivables, payment terms, inventory and the cash they tie up.

  • Growth economics

    What each marketing channel and customer type really costs, and what it returns.

03What it does

The analysis a good consultant would do, done in days.

AI reads every transaction and builds the models. A person checks the findings and makes the recommendations.

  • Cost review

    Categorizes and trends every expense. Flags price creep, duplicate tools and spending that has grown faster than revenue.

  • Margin by job, product and customer

    Shows which work makes money and which quietly loses it, down to the line item.

  • Pricing analysis

    Finds discounting patterns, underpriced services and costs that have outrun prices, with scenarios for adjusting.

  • Process mapping

    Uses system data and short staff interviews to show where time goes and where work stalls.

  • Automation sizing

    Estimates hours per task, cost per hour and what an agent could take on, ranked by value.

  • Cash and working capital

    Receivables aging, payment terms and inventory turns, and what tightening them would free up.

  • Scenario modeling

    Tests “what if” questions, like a price change, a new hire or a lost customer, with the assumptions visible.

  • Prioritized roadmap

    Every opportunity ranked by expected savings, effort and payback, with an owner and a first step.

  • AI strategy and readiness

    Where AI fits your business and where it doesn’t, the data and policies you need, and a plain AI use policy for staff.

05How it works

From your data to a roadmap, in weeks.

Typically 3 to 5 weeks from kickoff to readout. Most of that goes to gathering data and fitting short interviews into your team’s week. The analysis itself takes days, not weeks of billed hours.

01

Kickoff

We agree on goals, constraints and the questions that matter most, then send a short data request.

Typically week 1

02

Data in

You share 12 to 24 months of financial and operational data, read-only or as exports. We handle the cleanup.

Typically weeks 1 to 2

03

Interviews

Short conversations with the people who run the work, usually 30 minutes each.

Typically week 2

04

Analysis and review

AI does the heavy lifting across every transaction. A person checks every finding before it reaches you.

Typically weeks 2 to 4

05

Readout and roadmap

We walk you through the findings and the plan, and answer every question.

Typically weeks 3 to 5

Optional: we implement. Hand the roadmap to your team, or have us build the agents that deliver it. Your call.

Timing depends mostly on how quickly data can be shared.

09FAQ

Questions about AI Consulting.

Why does it cost so much less than a consulting firm?

Because much of a traditional fee pays for people reading your data by hand. AI does that part in days. You still get an experienced person checking every finding and building the plan, and you pay a fixed fee for the review, not an hourly bill.

How is this different from the free assessment?

The free assessment is a 30-minute call built on what you tell us, including the short intake form: your top three opportunities and a rough, modeled estimate. AI Consulting spends 3 to 5 weeks inside your books and operating data: every opportunity sized from your own transactions and ranked by payback, a 90-day plan, a 12-month roadmap and the working model. The assessment tells you whether a review is worth doing, and what the fixed fee would be, before you commit to anything.

How is this different from what my accountant does?

Your accountant keeps the books accurate, compliant and filed. We use those books, plus your operational data, to look forward: where you’re losing margin, what to fix first and what it’s worth. We’re happy to work alongside your accountant.

What data do you need?

Typically 12 to 24 months of profit and loss and balance sheet, a general ledger or transaction export, payroll summaries, vendor and customer lists, and any job, order or time data you have. Read-only access or exports both work. We send a checklist.

How long does it take?

Typically 3 to 5 weeks from kickoff to readout. Most of that is gathering data and scheduling short interviews; the analysis itself takes days. The biggest factor is how quickly data can be shared.

Do we have to use you to implement the roadmap?

No. The roadmap and the model are yours. Some recommendations need agents we can build. Others are a phone call to a vendor. You choose who does what.

Is AI really doing the analysis?

AI does the heavy lifting: reading every transaction, categorizing spend, spotting patterns and running the models. A person checks every finding, adds business judgment and makes the recommendations. You get the speed of one and the judgment of the other.
