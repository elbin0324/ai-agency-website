02How they work together

One set of rules, however many agents you run.

Every agent works inside the tools you already use, follows the same guardrails and brings anything sensitive to you. A second service adds capacity, not a second system to manage.

Included with every service

  • Discovery and design

    We map your workflow with the people who do it before we build anything.

  • Integration

    Connected to the tools you already use, read-only wherever possible.

  • Testing on your past work

    Agents prove themselves on recent work before they go live.

  • Guardrails

    Approval rules, hard limits and handoff rules you set.

  • Monitoring and upkeep

    We watch, tune and fix as part of the work, not as a separate ticket.

  • Monthly reporting

    Results in hours and dollars, against a baseline agreed up front.

  • A named lead

    One person who knows your business and answers your questions.

  • Documentation you keep

    How each agent works, what it can access and the rules it follows.
