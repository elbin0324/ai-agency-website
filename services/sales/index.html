03Research checklist

What the agent checks before it writes a word.

Every prospect gets a short dossier. If the research doesn’t turn up a real reason to reach out, the prospect is skipped.

  1. 01

    Fit.

    Matches your ideal customer profile: industry, size, location and the signals you care about.

  2. 02

    Not already in your world.

    Not a current customer, an open deal, a past opt-out or on your do-not-contact list.

  3. 03

    Their website.

    What they sell, who they serve, where they operate and what’s new.

  4. 04

    Recent news.

    Openings, expansions, new services, leadership changes and announcements.

  5. 05

    Hiring.

    Open roles that reveal priorities or strain, like a first operations hire or one role covering three jobs.

  6. 06

    The person.

    Their role, responsibilities and tenure, plus any public, work-related posts.

  7. 07

    Customer reviews.

    Recurring themes in public reviews, good and bad.

  8. 08

    Tools they use.

    What their website and job posts reveal about the systems they run on.

  9. 09

    A reason to write now.

    One specific, verifiable trigger tied to your offer.

  10. 10

    A verified address.

    The email address is validated before anything is sent.

Our rule

No reason, no email.

Every fact in an email links back to its source in the dossier, so you can check it in one click. Research covers business context only, never personal details.

04Our quality bar

Every email has to earn its send.

Before anything goes out, the draft is checked against these eight standards. Anything that falls short goes to a person, or doesn’t go at all.

  • True.

    Every fact is checked against its source. Nothing invented, nothing exaggerated.

  • Specific.

    At least one observation the reader would recognize as accurate about their business.

  • Relevant.

    Tied to an offer you’ve approved and a problem they plausibly have.

  • Short.

    Under 120 words before the signature. Plain text. No images or attachments.

  • One clear ask.

    Easy to say yes to. Usually something useful, not a meeting.

  • Your voice.

    Matches your tone and style guide. No filler openers, no fake familiarity.

  • Honest.

    No fake “Re:” subject lines, no false urgency and no misleading claims.

  • Respectful.

    Business context only. Nothing about anyone’s personal life, family or sensitive traits.

The review queue shows the result of every check, for every email.

05What it does

A researcher, writer and sales assistant in one.

It does the slow, careful parts of prospecting that busy teams skip, for every prospect.

  • Builds your list

    Finds businesses that match your ideal customer profile, from reputable business data sources and public information.

  • Researches every prospect

    Builds a short dossier from their website, news, job posts, reviews and the person’s role.

  • Finds the reason to write

    Looks for a real, recent trigger that connects to what you sell, and skips the prospect if there isn’t one.

  • Writes one email for one person

    Short, specific and in your voice, with one clear, easy ask.

  • Checks quality before sending

    Verifies facts against sources and checks tone, length, claims and spam-trigger phrasing.

  • Sends on your rules

    Approve every email, approve a sample, or let it send anything above the quality bar. Daily limits per inbox, always.

  • Follows up like a person

    A few follow-ups, each adding something new. It stops the moment someone replies or opts out.

  • Handles replies

    Sorts interested, not now, wrong person and unsubscribe. Drafts responses, books meetings and flags hot replies.

  • Logs everything in your CRM

    Contacts, research notes, emails, replies and next steps in HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive or a spreadsheet.

07Deliverability and compliance

Built to reach the inbox and respect the rules.

A great email is wasted in a spam folder, and no result is worth your reputation. Both are handled from day one.

Deliverability

  • A separate sending domain. Outreach goes from a dedicated domain or subdomain, so your main domain’s reputation stays protected.
  • Authenticated email. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are set up and tested before the first send, to the standards Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders.
  • Gradual warm-up. New inboxes ramp up slowly over several weeks.
  • Low daily limits. Sends are capped per inbox and spread through the business day.
  • Verified addresses only. Every address is checked before sending, and bounces are removed automatically.
  • Plain, human formatting. Plain text, no images and few or no links.
  • Daily health checks. Bounce, complaint and reply rates are watched every day. Sending pauses automatically if something looks off.

Compliance

  • CAN-SPAM basics, every time. Accurate sender details, honest subject lines, your physical mailing address and a clear way to opt out.
  • Opt-outs honored automatically. A “no thanks” or an unsubscribe stops all future emails to that person, across every inbox, right away.
  • Your do-not-contact list respected. Synced with your CRM, so customers, open deals and past opt-outs never get cold outreach.
  • Business context only. Research uses public, work-related information. Nothing personal or sensitive.
  • Stricter regions scoped first. Canada (CASL), the UK and the EU (GDPR) have stricter rules. We scope those markets with you and your counsel, or leave them out.
  • Records kept. Every send, reply and opt-out is logged.

09FAQ

Questions about the Sales Agent.

Isn’t cold email just spam?

Spam is irrelevant, deceptive and sent in bulk. Our outreach is researched, relevant to the reader, honest about who’s writing, easy to opt out of and sent in small numbers. If the agent can’t find a genuine reason to contact someone, it doesn’t.

Will it send from my main email address?

No. We set up a separate sending domain or subdomain, so outreach stays apart from your main domain’s reputation. Replies come straight to you or your sales team.

How many emails does it send?

Deliberately few. Sends are capped per inbox per day and ramped up slowly. We’d rather send fewer emails that get read than many that get deleted.

Can I approve emails before they go out?

Yes. You can approve every email, approve a sample, or let the agent send anything that passes the quality bar. A good way to start is approving every email, then relaxing that once you trust the drafts.

Where do the prospects come from? Do you buy lists?

We don’t blast purchased lists. The agent builds each list for you from reputable business data sources and public information, checks every prospect against your criteria and your do-not-contact list, and verifies every address.

What results should we expect?

Meetings, not a flood of replies. Even well-researched cold email is a slow channel: we model about 0.7 to 1.5 held meetings for every 1,000 researched prospects, from published benchmarks. It pays off when one new account is worth a lot to you, as it usually is for distributors, commercial services and B2B firms.
