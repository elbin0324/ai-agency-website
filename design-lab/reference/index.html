    08FAQ

    Straight answers to common questions.

    Don't see yours? Ask us on the free assessment call.

    How is this different from using ChatGPT ourselves?

    Chat tools are good at helping one person with one question. An agent does a job. It's connected to your systems, follows your rules, takes action and knows when to hand off to a person.

    Do we need technical staff or new software?

    No. We do the technical work, and agents work inside the tools you already use. What we need is a decision-maker who can arrange access and review the agent's early work.

    Is our data safe?

    Least-privilege access that's read-only by default, human approval for sensitive actions, audit logs of everything agents do, and business-grade AI providers whose terms exclude training on your data.

    Do you have case studies or references?

    Not yet, and we won't invent them. Instead we show working demos, worked examples with the math visible, and a pilot with a success measure agreed in writing.
